An entry for make.fun's "One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800" bounty: a single sentence, at most 200 characters, that explains the modern internet to a traveler from the year 1800 — a picture they could hold in their head, told in words of their own time.

The sentence pictures the internet as a web of copper wires spanning every kingdom, along which a letter, a likeness, or a whole library may fly swifter than lightning and be read by all the world at once.

`main.ts` is a small Node program (program/v1) that writes the sentence to `entry.txt` in the output directory (`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, default `/output`), with no trailing newline. `line.txt` holds the sentence exactly as graded.
