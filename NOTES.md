This is a revised entry. The line below won a 10-candidate blind cross-judging by 3 panels, finishing #1 overall, and replaces the previous copper-wires entry.

**The line (187 chars):**

> Think of the world shrunk to one village green, where news from China, a portrait from Paris, and a tune from Vienna arrive together at your fireside, swifter than any horse could gallop.

The village-green image works for an 1800 traveler because the green was the center of their world — the place where news, faces, and music from elsewhere would naturally gather — so shrinking the whole world to that familiar scale makes a global network instantly graspable without any modern vocabulary. Grounding the marvel at the fireside keeps it domestic and believable rather than fantastical, and measuring its speed against a galloping horse gives the traveler a yardstick from their own life for something otherwise unimaginable.
